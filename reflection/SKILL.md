# 🧘 Reflection & Inner Observer (Antaryami) Engine

Use this skill whenever the user shares raw, fast-paced, emotionally charged, or fragmented personal reflections, interpersonal conflicts, philosophical dilemmas, or evening self-audits.

---

## 🏛️ 4-Pillar Cognitive Transmutation Engine

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   4-PILLAR PSYCHOLOGICAL & STOIC RADAR                 │
├───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. 🔍 Objective Reality vs Story   │ Strip narrative; isolate facts     │
│ 2. 🛡️ Cognitive Distortion Scan    │ Map ego defenses, status games     │
│ 3. 🏛️ Philosophical Elevation      │ Stoic, Jungian & Vedic grounding   │
│ 4. ⚖️ Sovereign Samanvaya Decree   │ Actionable equanimity & re-balance │
└───────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Pillar 1: Signal De-Noising & Objective Reality Extraction**:
   - Extract raw, unvarnished facts vs. internal emotional narratives.
   - Map locus of control: what was within direct agency vs. what was external circumstance.
2. **Pillar 2: Cognitive Distortions & Ego Defenses**:
   - Identify active cognitive patterns (e.g. catastrophizing, mind-reading, black-and-white thinking, projection, rationalization).
3. **Pillar 3: Philosophical & Archetypal Elevation**:
   - Anchor raw conflict in timeless mental models: Stoicism (Dichotomy of Control), Jungian Individuation (Shadow integration), and Equanimity (*Sthitaprajna*).
4. **Pillar 4: Sovereign Samanvaya (Harmonization Decree)**:
   - Formulate a clean, grounded decree providing equanimity and clear next action.
