# Anti-Cliché Visual Design & AI UI Tropes Checklist

LLM-generated interfaces frequently fall into predictable aesthetic traps. Use this checklist to audit and eliminate generic tropes.

---

## ❌ Top 5 Generic AI UI Tropes

1. **The SaaS Card Cliché**:
   - *Trope*: Breaking every piece of content into identical rounded cards with identical 8px blur drop-shadows.
   - *Fix*: Use editorial whitespace, asymmetric column grids, and subtle border lines instead of card containers everywhere.

2. **Neon Acid Gradients on Dark Mode**:
   - *Trope*: Pure `#000` pitch-black backgrounds with blinding cyan or neon-purple gradients.
   - *Fix*: Use rich tinted canvas tones (e.g. deep slate `#0e1217`, warm charcoal `#141416`) with a single, deliberate accent.

3. **Tracked-Out All-Caps Eyebrows**:
   - *Trope*: `FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS` or `OVERVIEW` in tiny uppercase letters above every headline.
   - *Fix*: Rely on natural typographic contrast (size, weight, spacing) rather than decorative eyebrow labels.

4. **Noisy Scattered Animations**:
   - *Trope*: Every card sliding up and fading in on scroll, combined with hover scale triggers on every element.
   - *Fix*: Restrict motion to user-triggered state changes (opening drawers, expanding modals, confirming actions).

5. **Cliché Typography Pairings**:
   - *Trope*: Default Inter or Roboto for everything without intentional weight/tracking adjustments.
   - *Fix*: Pair an intentional display typeface (editorial serif or characterful geometric sans) with a clean, readable text face.
